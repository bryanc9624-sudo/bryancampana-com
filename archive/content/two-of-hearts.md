Bryan Campana
Work
Two of Hearts
is about the delicate balance between human presence and the built environment. I’m exploring the contrast between the enormity and rigidity of urban architecture and the fleeting interactions that happen within them.
Our presence may be small in the face of these monumental structures, but essential in giving life to stone and shadow.

2021
Medium
