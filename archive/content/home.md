Bryan Campana
Work
Bryan Campana is a graphic designer working in visual systems: typography, information hierarchy, and the standards that keep a body of work consistent.
He is currently an Environmental Graphic Designer at TPG Architecture, developing signage and wayfinding for workplace and corporate interiors. Before that he spent two years at Dura Architectural Signage, translating design intent into fabrication and building the documentation systems that made it repeatable.
He also keeps an art practice in photography and printmaking. Based in Queens, New York.
