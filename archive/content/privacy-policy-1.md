Privacy Policy
Last Updated: January 1, 2025
This Privacy Policy describes how personal information is collected, used, and shared when you interact with bryancampana.com.
Information Collected
If you use the contact form, I may collect your name, email address, or any other information you provide. When you purchase products, order details are collected and securely processed by Stripe. I do not store or process payment card details directly. Basic technical data such as browser type, device information, and anonymized traffic statistics may also be collected to improve performance.
Sharing of Information
I do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Order and payment information is shared with Stripe to complete transactions. For details, review
Stripe’s Privacy Policy
.
Use of Information
Personal information is used to respond to inquiries, process and fulfill orders, and improve the website.
Your Rights
You may request the deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting me.
