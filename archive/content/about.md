About
Bryan Campana is a graphic designer working in visual systems: typography, information hierarchy, and documentation standards.
He is currently an Environmental Graphic Designer at TPG Architecture, developing signage and wayfinding for workplace and corporate interiors. The work covers sign type design, location plans, message schedules, and the drawings that fabricators and installers build from.
Before TPG he spent two years at Dura Architectural Signage, a fabricator, translating architects’ design intent into shop drawings and building the templates and drawing standards the shop worked from.
He also keeps an art practice in photography and printmaking. His work has been shown at Treat Gallery, Dodomu Gallery, and the New Media Artspace.
B.A. in Graphic Design, minor in New Media Arts, Baruch College (CUNY). Lives and works in Queens, New York.
